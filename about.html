About the Wisconsin Elections Archive

This website provides easy access to Wisconsin election results at different levels of aggregation. Records for presidential, gubernatorial, congressional, and state supreme court elections are available beginning 2000. State legislative elections are available beginning in 2010.

Results can be viewed with two kinds of integration, selectable in the sidebar.

Nominal integration shows the election results for each geography as that geography existed during the election in question. These are official, certified election results. When set to nominal integration (the default setting), the map uses boundary files corresponding to the boundaries in use at the time of the election.

Spatial integration allocates past election results into the reporting unit boundaries used during the August 2026 election, based on validated voter overlap. These can differ from official, certified results but they allow users to compare apples-to-apples geographies over time. Spatially integrated results are available for statewide contests only; sub-county levels cover elections from 2016 onward. Because county boundaries have not changed for many decades in Wisconsin, the nominally and spatially integrated election results are identical for county-level election results.

All election results were collected and standardized by John Johnson. The source code and data may be viewed and downloaded here.
