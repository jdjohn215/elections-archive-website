About the Wisconsin Elections Archive

This website provides easy access to Wisconsin election results at different levels of aggregation. Records for presidential, gubernatorial, congressional, and state supreme court elections are available beginning 2000. State legislative elections are available beginning in 2010.

Results can be viewed with two kinds of integration, selectable in the sidebar. Nominal integration shows the election results for each geography as that geography existed during the election in question; the map uses boundary files corresponding to the boundaries in use during each election. Spatial integration allocates past election results into the reporting unit boundaries used during the August 2026 election, based on validated voter overlap. Spatially integrated results are available for statewide contests only; sub-county levels cover elections from 2016 onward, while county totals extend back to 2000.

All election results were collected and standardized by John Johnson. The source code and data may be viewed here.

This website is a project of the Marquette University Law School's Lubar Center for Public Policy Research and Civic Education.
